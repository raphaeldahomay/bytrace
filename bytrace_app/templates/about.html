Bytrace was born from a simple fascination: binary numbers. Behind every website, application, video, photograph, or artificial intelligence model lies the same language built on only two states: 0 and 1. One off, one on. One false, one true.

What I find remarkable is that with only these two symbols, humanity has built the digital world we live in today. From the first computers to modern AI systems, everything ultimately traces back to billions of binary decisions happening every second.

This project is both a tribute to that elegant simplicity and the first step of my own journey into computer science. Bytrace aims to make these concepts a little more tangible, showing how human-readable information can be translated into the raw language understood by machines.

Sometimes the most extraordinary systems are built from the simplest foundations.

— Raphaël Dahomay
